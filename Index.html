Decision & output

GTM Playbook — Germany

The entry decision and the recommendations.

Market context: Germany
Recommended entry model

Hybrid: direct in mid-tier, distributor-assisted elsewhere

Own the mid-tier accounts where the service wedge wins and margins justify a direct team. Use distributor reach for the long tail and university access while references build.

Commercial & pricing architecture
  • As-a-service option to remove the capex barrier
  • Uptime-guaranteed service SLA as the headline
  • Transparent, unbundled consumables pricing
  • NUB support playbook offered per account
Target-account sequence
  • Wave 1 — mid-tier knee-heavy, low lock-in
  • Wave 2 — regional chains, reference-led
  • Wave 3 — university hospitals once evidence exists
Launch & retention
  • KOL & clinical-evidence plan from day one
  • Service model as the retention engine
  • Account-expansion built into first contracts
Key recommendations
  • Enter, but service-led and mid-tier first
  • Compete on uptime & after-sales, not list price
  • Validate pricing & switching at Gate 2 before scale

Selection criteria
Market reputation — years in business, client references
Prior experience with Evalueserve on similar studies
Membership of ESOMAR or equivalent governing bodies
Capability — network reach across target geographies
Quality controls
Partner does not sub-outsource our work
For online panels: validated respondents, no double opt-ins
Reach and fit against the exact target audience
Confidence in delivering the defined sample and timeline

Project process measures

  • Unbiased instruments; the client's name is never revealed to respondents
  • For CI work, multiple brands are referenced to avoid bias
  • Clean-desk and clear-screen policy; least-privilege access
  • Information barriers ("Chinese walls") and strict staffing rules

Vendor & KOL onboarding

  • Trusted third-party vendors with the strongest credentials
  • NDAs signed so sensitive information is never misused
  • Master Service Agreements for transparent, legally-bound relationships
  • KOL screening for genuine, multi-OEM, senior experience

Data management & analysis

  • Findings reported on an aggregated basis; individual responses never disclosed
  • Secured data storage and encrypted transmission
  • Access limited to the project / account team only
  • GDPR-compliant retention and deletion

Industry standards

  • ESOMAR guidelines for ethical, self-regulated research
  • SCIP code of ethics for competitive-intelligence conduct
  • ISO 27001 certification for information security
  • GDPR for personal-data protection
